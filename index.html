Who I Am

Engineer by training. Builder by instinct. Planner by purpose.

I am Nitesh Pal — a Mechanical and Planning Engineer from Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, India, currently deployed in Lagos, Nigeria. My career began on the ground at the IOCL Panipat Refinery — supervising crude pump installations, inspecting weld joints, erecting pipe spools, and enforcing HSE protocols on a live refinery site.

That seven months of site experience is what sets me apart. I don't estimate durations from manuals — I know them because I've physically done the work. That intelligence drives every schedule, QS check, and look-ahead plan I produce as a Planning Engineer at Offshore Infrastructure Ltd., Lagos.

Academically I graduated as the Chancellor's Gold Medalist from CSA University of Agriculture & Technology, Kanpur. I hold a granted patent for a Multi-Purpose Solar-Based Corn Sheller backed by three published research papers.
